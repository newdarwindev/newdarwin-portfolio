Four ways into the work

RECORDED FIXTURES

Runspindle

Keep the agents you already use. Show what happens when a recurring job gets interrupted, needs a decision, or finishes but loses confirmation.

Best starting point: One recurring job and a result somebody can check.

MEETING CONCEPT + FIXTURE EVIDENCE

Meeting Lab

Put two isolated workspaces in view so a team can see the same evidence and decide what to do next. Keep viewing distinct from controlling.

02 / PROOF

What you can verify today

Recorded fixture
Runspindle’s published gallery and reel use synthetic business workflows. The recordings are real; the accounts and jobs are fictional.
Tested component
Browser lost-confirmation test checks the destination independently and confirms one effect instead of blindly repeating it. It does not establish production account access.
Reproducible work
Runspindle Lab keeps fixture, component and product integration evidence separate. A passing fixture cannot substitute for a real product run.
Research and design
Authorized Agent Workflows explains the design problem: how to separate what an agent can observe, what it may do, and what it can verify.

How the Runspindle parts fit

Tether carries the request and result. Core records intent and recoverable state; Host ties the work to a machine and session. Browser handles authorized web actions; Console shows status and evidence. Refinery evaluates changes and Platform supports isolated execution. See the exact component pins. A newer component test does not qualify the full composition.
